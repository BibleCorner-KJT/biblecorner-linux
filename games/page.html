❓ Who Am I?

Read the clue and guess the Bible person. Need help? Ask for another clue!

  1. I built a very big boat. Animals came to me two by two. After the rain, God showed me a rainbow.
  2. I was a shepherd boy. I played music on my harp. With God's help, I defeated a giant with a stone.
  3. As a baby, I was found in a basket by a river. God spoke to me from a burning bush. I led God's people through the Red Sea.
  4. God asked me to go to Nineveh, but I ran away. I was caught in a great storm at sea. A great fish swallowed me!
  5. I prayed to God three times every day. The king threw me into a den of lions. God shut the lions' mouths.
  6. The angel Gabriel visited me. I said yes to God. I am the Mother of Jesus.
  7. I was a fisherman. I walked on the water towards Jesus. I said three times that I did not know Jesus, but He forgave me.
  8. I was a very short man. I climbed a tree to see Jesus. Jesus came to my house for dinner.
  9. I was a bishop long ago. I secretly gave gold to a poor family. The Church remembers me on 6 December.
  10. I was the very first man. God made me from the dust of the ground. I lived in the Garden of Eden.
  11. I was the first woman. I lived in a beautiful garden. A serpent tricked me into eating the fruit.
  12. God told me to leave my home and travel to a new land. God said my family would be as many as the stars. My son's name was Isaac.
  13. I laughed when I heard I would have a baby. I was very old when my son was born. My husband was Abraham.
  14. My name means laughter. My parents were very old when I was born. My father was Abraham.
  15. I dreamed of a ladder reaching up to heaven. I had twelve sons. God gave me the new name Israel.
  16. My father gave me a coat of many colours. My brothers sold me, and I went to Egypt. I explained Pharaoh's dreams.
  17. I watched over my baby brother in a basket. I played a timbrel and sang after crossing the sea. My brothers were Moses and Aaron.
  18. I was the brother of Moses. I helped Moses speak to Pharaoh. I became the first high priest.
  19. I led God's people into the Promised Land. I sent spies into Jericho. The walls of Jericho fell down when we marched and shouted.
  20. I said, “Where you go, I will go.” I gathered grain in the fields of Boaz. I became the great-grandmother of King David.
  21. I prayed in the temple for a baby. God answered my prayer and I had a son. My son Samuel served God from when he was little.
  22. I was a boy who served in God's house. God called my name in the night. I said, “Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.”
  23. I was a king and the son of David. I asked God for wisdom. I built the great temple in Jerusalem.
  24. Ravens brought me bread and meat. God answered my prayer with fire from heaven. I went up to heaven in a chariot of fire.
  25. I was a Jewish girl who became a queen. I bravely spoke to the king to save my people. Maybe I was made queen for such a time as this.
  26. I lost almost everything I had. I kept trusting God even when I suffered. God blessed me with twice as much in the end.
  27. An angel found me hiding while I threshed wheat. I asked God for a sign with a fleece of wool. God won the battle with only 300 of my men.
  28. God gave me great strength. I once defeated a lion with my bare hands. My strength was connected to my long hair.
  29. I was a giant soldier. I shouted at God's people every day. A shepherd boy defeated me with a stone.
  30. I lived in the wilderness and wore camel's hair. I ate locusts and wild honey. I baptised Jesus in the River Jordan.
  31. I was the cousin of the Virgin Mary. My baby leapt for joy when Mary visited. My son was John the Baptist.
  32. I was a carpenter. An angel spoke to me in dreams. I cared for Mary and the child Jesus.
  33. I waited many years to see the Saviour. I held baby Jesus in the temple. I said, “Lord, now let your servant depart in peace.”
  34. I was a fisherman. I brought my brother Peter to meet Jesus. I was the first one Jesus called to follow Him.
  35. I used to collect taxes. Jesus said to me, “Follow me,” and I left my table. I wrote the first Gospel.
  36. I was one of the twelve apostles. I said I would not believe until I saw Jesus' wounds. When I saw the risen Jesus, I said, “My Lord and my God!”
  37. I used to be called Saul. A bright light shone on me on the road to Damascus. I travelled far and wrote many letters to the churches.
  38. I lived in Bethany with my sisters. I was in the tomb for four days. Jesus called, “Come out!” and I came back to life.
  39. I lived in Bethany with my sister and brother. I was busy cooking when Jesus visited. My brother was Lazarus.
  40. I followed Jesus and helped Him. I went to the tomb very early on Sunday. I was the first to see the risen Jesus.
  41. I was blind and sat by the road. I shouted, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” Jesus healed me and I could see.
  42. I was a teacher of the law. I came to talk with Jesus at night. Jesus told me about being born again.
  43. I was one of the first deacons. I was full of grace and power. I was the first Christian martyr.
  44. I was a young helper of Saint Paul. My grandmother Lois and mother Eunice taught me the Scriptures. Saint Paul wrote two letters to me.
  45. I sold purple cloth. I listened to Paul by the river. I was baptised in Philippi with my family.
  46. I was a brave soldier. I would not stop following Jesus. Icons show me on a horse defeating a dragon.
  47. I was a shepherd before I became a bishop. I lived on the island of Cyprus. My holy relics rest on the island of Corfu.
  48. I was a bishop in Caesarea. I built a place to care for the poor and sick. A special bread is baked for my feast on 1 January.
  49. I was a humble bishop in Greece. I lived on the island of Aegina. I wrote the hymn “O Virgin Pure”.
  50. I lived in a forest in Russia. I fed a bear by hand. I greeted everyone with “My joy, Christ is risen!”
  51. I was a monk from Russia. I travelled far across the sea to Alaska. I cared for the children and taught them about God.
  52. I was very clever and wise. I explained the faith to fifty learned men. A great monastery at Mount Sinai carries my name.
  53. I was the mother of the emperor Constantine. I travelled to Jerusalem. I found the Holy Cross of Christ.
  54. I was a Roman emperor. I saw a cross in the sky with the words “In this sign, conquer”. I built the city of Constantinople.
  55. I was a doctor. I healed the sick without asking for money. My name means “all-merciful”.
  56. Jesus told a story about me. I found a hurt man lying by the road. I bandaged him and paid for his room at an inn.
  57. Jesus told a story about me. I spent all my money and ended up feeding pigs. My father ran to hug me when I came home.
  58. I was the Roman governor. I washed my hands in front of the crowd. I asked Jesus, “What is truth?”
  59. We came from the East. We followed a bright star. We brought gold, frankincense and myrrh.
  60. I am an archangel. I told Zechariah he would have a son. I brought the good news to the Virgin Mary.
  61. I am an archangel. My name means “Who is like God?” Icons show me leading the heavenly army.
  62. The Lord filled me with His Spirit, with wisdom and skill in every kind of craft. I was from the tribe of Judah, the son of Uri. I made the Ark of the Covenant for the Tabernacle.
  63. I was a prophetess who lived in Jerusalem. My husband kept the royal wardrobe. King Josiah's men came to ask me about the Book of the Law they had found.
  64. I was a judge of Israel, and I was left-handed. I made a double-edged sword about a cubit long. I defeated Eglon, the very fat king of Moab.
  65. I lived in a tent. A thirsty man asked for water, and I gave him milk. Deborah sang that I was most blessed among women, because I defeated Sisera.
  66. I was an Ethiopian official in the king's palace. I used old rags and ropes to rescue a prophet. I pulled Jeremiah out of a muddy cistern.
  67. I was a Pharisee and a teacher of the Law, honoured by all the people. I told the council: if this work is from God, you will not be able to stop it. Saul of Tarsus studied at my feet.
  68. I was lame in both feet. My nurse dropped me when I was five, as she fled in a hurry. King David let me eat at his table always, for my father Jonathan's sake.
  69. I lived in Joppa and was always doing good. I made coats and clothes for the widows. Peter prayed, and I was raised from the dead.
  70. I sold purple cloth. I came from the city of Thyatira. I was baptised at Philippi and opened my home to Paul.
  71. I was a Jew from Alexandria, eloquent and strong in the Scriptures. Priscilla and Aquila explained the way of God to me more fully. Paul wrote that he planted, I watered, but God gave the growth.
  72. I was a young man in Troas. I fell asleep while Paul talked late into the night. I fell from a third-floor window, and Paul brought me back to life.
  73. I was a servant girl in the house of Mary, the mother of John Mark. I was so happy to hear Peter's voice that I forgot to open the gate. The others told me I was out of my mind.
  74. I was a priest of Midian. My daughter Zipporah married Moses. I advised Moses to choose leaders to help him judge the people.
  75. I hid a beautiful robe, silver and gold in the ground under my tent. Because of my sin, Israel was defeated at Ai. I lived in the time of Joshua, after the walls of Jericho fell.
  76. I was wise and beautiful, but my husband Nabal was foolish. I brought bread, wine, raisins and figs to David's men. My words stopped David from taking revenge.
  77. I was the servant of the prophet Elisha. I ran after Naaman and lied to get silver and clothes. Naaman's leprosy came upon me.
  78. I was in charge of King Ahab's palace, and I feared the Lord greatly. I hid one hundred prophets in two caves. I fed them bread and water while Jezebel was killing the prophets.
  79. I was a rich man and a member of the council. I was waiting for the Kingdom of God, and I did not agree with what the council did. I asked Pilate for the body of Jesus and laid it in my own new tomb.
  80. The Holy Spirit told me I would not die before I had seen the Lord's Christ. I took the baby Jesus in my arms in the Temple. I prayed: Lord, now let Your servant depart in peace.
  81. I was a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. I was very old and never left the Temple, worshipping with fasting and prayer night and day. I gave thanks to God and spoke about the baby Jesus to everyone.
  82. I was a disciple living in Damascus. The Lord told me to go to a street called Straight. I laid my hands on Saul, and he could see again.
  83. I was a centurion of the Italian Regiment in Caesarea. An angel told me to send for Simon Peter. My household and I were among the first Gentiles to receive the Holy Spirit and be baptised.
  84. My name means son of encouragement. I sold a field and brought the money to the apostles. I travelled with Paul on his first missionary journey.
  85. My father Enoch walked with God. My grandson was Noah. I lived longer than anyone else in the Bible: 969 years.
  86. I was from the tribe of Levi. I hid my baby son for three months. I made a basket of papyrus and laid my son in it among the reeds of the Nile.
  87. I was a wealthy man of Bethlehem. I told my workers to leave extra grain for a young widow from Moab. I married Ruth and became the great-grandfather of King David.
  88. Every year I went up to Shiloh and prayed for a son. Eli the priest thought I was drunk, because my lips moved without a sound. I gave my son Samuel to serve the Lord.
  89. I became king when I was only eight years old. In my reign the Book of the Law was found in the Temple. When I heard its words I tore my clothes and led the people back to God.
  90. I prayed when Sennacherib threatened Jerusalem. God added fifteen years to my life. As a sign for me, the shadow went back ten steps on the stairway of Ahaz.
  91. I served the church at Cenchreae. Paul asked the Christians in Rome to welcome me in the Lord. Paul said I had been a helper of many, and of him too.
  92. I was a leader among the brothers in Jerusalem. I was beaten and put in prison with Paul at Philippi. At midnight we sang hymns, and an earthquake opened the prison doors.
  93. I was one of the twelve spies sent into Canaan. I said: let us go up at once and take the land, for we are well able. At eighty-five I asked for the hill country of Hebron.
  94. I was commander of the army of the king of Aram. A little servant girl from Israel told my wife about a prophet. I washed seven times in the Jordan and was healed of leprosy.
  95. I became king of Judah at sixteen and was famous for my towers and machines. I grew proud and went into the Temple to burn incense, which only priests may do. Leprosy broke out on my forehead in front of the priests.
  96. I was a Hebrew midwife in Egypt. Pharaoh told me to let the Hebrew baby boys die, but I feared God. God was kind to me and gave me a family of my own.
